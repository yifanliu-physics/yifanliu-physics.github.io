Ph.D. Student in Physics

Yifan Liu

Institute for Solid State Physics, The University of Tokyo

My research explores universal phenomena in quantum many-body systems through the lens of (boundary) conformal field theory. I develop analytical field-theoretic descriptions and entanglement-based diagnostics that bridge microscopic lattice models and their continuum descriptions, with numerical tests carried out in collaboration. More broadly, I am interested in using quantum field theory to understand critical phenomena and dynamics in lattice systems, with particular interests in boundary criticality, quantum-information measures of many-body states, and non-Hermitian critical phenomena and biorthogonal quench dynamics.

Research Interests
